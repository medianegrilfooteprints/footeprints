Room questions9
How do room prices work, and can children stay?

Our rates are per room, not per person, for two adults, with continental breakfast, plus taxes and fees. Each room above shows its price per night.

Children 0–12 stay at no extra charge. Most rooms take 2 adults and 1 child. Rooms with two double beds (Hotel Suite Double, Ocean View Double and Pool Walk-out Double) take 2 adults and 2 children. A third adult (13 and over) is $25 per night, taxes included.

See the rooms
Is FootePrints all-inclusive?

We’re a boutique hotel rather than an all-inclusive resort, and continental breakfast is included with every room.

If you’d like everything covered, add our optional All-Inclusive Plan: $165 per adult and $100 per child aged 4–12, per night, for the whole stay.

Rooms and the plan
What should I know before I arrive?

Check-in is from 3 PM and check-out is by 12 PM.

Late check-out is $60 until 3 PM or $120 until 6 PM, available when the hotel isn’t fully booked and your room isn’t needed for arriving guests. Add it to your room request, or ask the front desk.

We’re a cashless property, so please bring a card, and hotel guests have a $200 card pre-authorisation at check-in.

Why can’t I book some rooms online?

Our Oceanfront and Pool Walk-out rooms are only bookable with us, and the Deluxe Suites are booked by request. Tap Request by email on the room: you’ll see the full price for your dates before you send it, and our team replies within 24–48 hours.

Which rooms have a balcony?

Each of our four Oceanfront rooms has a balcony, and the Deluxe Hotel Suite has a large private one. For other rooms we can’t promise a balcony.

Do you have rooms for guests with limited mobility?

Yes. Our two Pool Walk-out rooms are on the ground floor, with no stairs to your door. Mention it when you book.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes, a private car from Montego Bay airport, taxes included: $125 one way or $250 round trip, for up to four people. Add it to your room request, or email us for a bigger group or a transfer from Kingston.

Email reservations
Is there a local rate for Jamaican residents?

Yes: 10% off the room rate for Jamaican residents. Tick I live in Jamaica in your room request and the price updates straight away.

Please have a valid Jamaican ID or proof of residency ready: our reservations team may ask for it before payment, and the front desk checks it again at check-in. Without it, the standard rate applies.

Is this the old Foote Prints on the Sands?

Yes. It is the same beachfront property on Seven Mile Beach, fully renovated and renamed Negril FootePrints Hotel.

Dining questions12
Do I need to stay at the hotel to eat here?

No. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are open to everyone. Walk in for breakfast or lunch at Blushy’s, or book a table for dinner at Gatsby’s.

Do I need a reservation for dinner?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking dinner at Gatsby’s, especially on holidays and special days.

Reserve at Gatsby’s
Is there a dress code?

At Gatsby’s in the evening: resort casual. No swimwear, cover-ups or bare feet, please.

Do you have a children’s menu?

Children are very welcome. A children’s menu is coming soon, and high chairs are available. Until then, our team is happy to help you choose.

Is there a happy hour?

Yes, every day from 4 to 6 PM at Blushy’s Bar & Grill: 2-for-1 on selected cocktails and martinis, Red Stripe and Blushy’s Special.

See the drinks menu
How many people can you seat together?

Up to 10 guests at one table. For a bigger group, contact us and we’ll plan it with you.

What if I have a food allergy?

Please mention it when you book on Eat App, and tell your server when you order.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes?

Yes. Dinner has a vegetarian section, and dishes are marked V (vegan) and G (gluten-free) on every menu.

Are tax and service included in the prices?

Menu prices are in US dollars and are subject to 10% government tax and 15% service charge.

How do I pay?

By card. FootePrints is a cashless property.

Can you seat a group of 6 or more?

Yes. Online booking on Eat App is for up to 5 guests. For 6 or more, send us a group request and we’ll set up your table and reply to confirm.

Should I tip my server?

Tipping is never expected. A 15% service charge is already on your bill.

If you enjoyed your meal and the service, you’re always welcome to leave a little extra for the team. They’ll be very grateful.

Day Pass questions8
Can I book a Day Pass for today?

Yes. Same-day bookings are welcome: reserve online, then come straight over.

Can I come without booking?

You can try, but we can’t promise you a spot. Day Pass spaces are limited each day, and the beach closes to day visitors on private event days, on fully booked days and on some holidays.

To be sure of your day, reserve online before you come.

What happens if it rains?

The Day Pass goes ahead rain or shine, and we don’t refund it for the weather once you’ve arrived.

Please check the forecast before you come. If it doesn’t look good, you can move your Day Pass to another date before your visit.

Is it cheaper if I arrive late or leave early?

No. The price is the same for the day, whether you arrive at 9 AM or 1 PM, and whether you leave at noon or at 5.

Can I book for a big group?

Yes, online like any other booking. You can add up to 10 people in one booking; for a bigger group, simply make a second booking for the same date.

Spaces are limited each day, so book early for a group.

If we eat at the restaurant, can we stay on the beach?

A meal at Blushy’s or Gatsby’s doesn’t include the beach or the pool.

To spend the day on the loungers, book a Day Pass, then order food and drinks to your chair.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Food and drinks are served by Blushy’s Bar & Grill, so please don’t bring your own.

Baby food and snacks for little ones are of course fine.

Do children need a ticket?

Yes, please add everyone to your booking: $20 for ages 4–12, and a free ticket for ages 0–3, so we know how many of you are coming.

Spa questions7
Do I need to stay at the hotel?

No. Audrey’s Spa is open to everyone: hotel guests, Day Pass guests and visitors.

Can I just walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is best, so we can keep a time for you.

Send a request with the Book a treatment button and our front desk will confirm your time.

Does the request confirm my booking?

Not yet. The form sends your wishes to our front desk; it doesn’t show live availability.

We reply to confirm your time, or suggest the closest one that’s free.

Do you have a couples massage?

Yes. Our Couples Massage is 60 minutes, $250, side by side in the same room.

You can also ask for any treatment for two people: choose For 2 people in the request form. When two of our therapists are free at your time, we’ll do it side by side.

Can I have a massage while pregnant?

Yes, we offer a Pregnancy Massage, 60 minutes, $110. Please mention it in your request.

Is the spa included in the Day Pass or the All-Inclusive Plan?

No. Spa treatments are booked and paid separately.

How do I pay?

By card, at the hotel. FootePrints is a cashless property. Prices are in US dollars, plus 10% government tax and 15% service charge: the request form shows your total before you send it.
