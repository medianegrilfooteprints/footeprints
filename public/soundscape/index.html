Soundscape questions

What is Soundscape?

A four-day beach club weekend at FootePrints on Seven Mile Beach in Negril, 28 – 31 May 2027: DJ sets and live music by the water, wellness in the mornings, good food all day, and relaxed beach club evenings into the night. Not a nightclub.

Tickets aren’t on sale yet: join the early list to hear first.

When is Soundscape?

28 – 31 May 2027, at FootePrints on Seven Mile Beach in Negril.

Are tickets on sale?

Not yet. Join the early list and we’ll tell you first when they open.

What’s the difference between the Stay + Access Package and the Access Pass?

The Stay + Access Package is three nights at FootePrints for two, with daily breakfast and full Soundscape access for four days. The Access Pass is four days of Soundscape for guests staying anywhere in Negril.

How much will it cost?

Prices will be announced when tickets go on sale. Everyone on the early list hears first.

Who’s playing?

We’ll share the line-up as soon as it’s confirmed. Join the early list to hear first.

Is it a party?

It’s a beach club weekend: DJs and live music by the sea, good food, wellness in the mornings and relaxed evenings. Not a nightclub.

Does joining the list mean I’ve booked?

No. It’s free, with no commitment. We’ll simply tell you when tickets open so you can decide.

All-Inclusive Plan

FootePrints is a boutique hotel, not an all-inclusive resort. Continental breakfast always comes with your room. The plan is an optional extra, for guests who’d like everything covered.

$165per adult (13+), per night
$100per child aged 4–12, per night
Freechildren 3 and under

What’s included

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner
  • Water, soft drinks, juices, coffee and tea
  • Beer, wine by the glass, cocktails, and all spirits by the glass
  • The minibar in your room, restocked once a day

Not included

  • Bottles of alcohol
  • Spa treatments
  • Airport transfers and excursions
  • Special experiences marked as extra

Good to know

  • The plan is for the whole stay, not for single days.
  • It is added for everyone staying in the room.
  • Children 3 and under are free when sharing the room with two adults on the plan.
